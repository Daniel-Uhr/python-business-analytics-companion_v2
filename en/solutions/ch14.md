Hummingbird Supermarkets compares 80 stores. The generator draws monthly complaints with a Poisson mean of two per thousand customers. Use floor area and customer traffic to assess the manager’s claim about service quality.

```python
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
pd.set_option("display.width", 160)
pd.set_option("display.max_columns", None)
import statsmodels.formula.api as smf

rng = np.random.default_rng(2026)
n_lojas = 80

area = rng.uniform(1, 4, size=n_lojas)
fluxo = 5 * area + rng.normal(0, 1.5, size=n_lojas)

lojas = pd.DataFrame({
    "area_thousand_m2": area.round(2),
    "funcionarios": np.round(8 + 9 * area + rng.normal(0, 3, size=n_lojas)).astype(int),
    "traffic_thousands": fluxo.round(1),
    "complaints": rng.poisson(2 * fluxo),
    "receita_mil": np.round(60 + 12 * fluxo + rng.normal(0, 25, size=n_lojas), 1),
})
```

```python
com_exposicao = smf.poisson("complaints ~ area_thousand_m2", data=lojas, exposure=lojas["traffic_thousands"]).fit(disp=0, cov_type="HC1")
```

## Solution to Exercise 04 — Conditional dispersion

Calculate Pearson dispersion for the exposure-adjusted model. Explain why the raw count variance can be much larger than its mean.

```python
import pandas as pd

import statsmodels.formula.api as smf

rng = np.random.default_rng(2026)
n_vendedores = 400
vendedores = pd.DataFrame({
    "badge": rng.binomial(1, 0.4, size=n_vendedores),
    "fashion": rng.binomial(1, 0.3, size=n_vendedores),

```
