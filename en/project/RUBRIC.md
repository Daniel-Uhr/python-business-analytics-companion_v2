# Assessment rubric — Final project



Total: 100 points. Code must run, but analytical decisions, interpretation, inference, and communication also matter.



## Understanding data and questions — 10 points



**Excellent:** Identifies unit, period, variable origins, and the distinction between choice, assignment, and records.



**Adequate:** Covers central elements with minor omissions.



**Partial:** Partly confuses structure or questions.



**Insufficient:** Analyzes without understanding data or questions.



## Data quality and preparation — 10 points



**Excellent:** Investigates missingness, zeros, extremes, and consistency; records choices, alternatives, and consequences.



**Adequate:** Addresses main problems with mostly sufficient justification.



**Partial:** Incomplete investigation or weak justification.



**Insufficient:** Ignores quality or changes data arbitrarily.



## Outcome and justified specification — 15 points



**Excellent:** Matches models to outcomes; justifies controls and form before p-values and recognizes changes of question.



**Adequate:** Appropriate specifications with sufficient reasoning.



**Partial:** Partly suitable or weakly justified specifications.



**Insufficient:** Incompatible model or p-value-driven choice.



## Estimation and inference — 15 points



**Excellent:** Correct estimation and appropriate uncertainty; identifies covariance and assumptions, reports and interprets intervals.



**Adequate:** Correct estimation and inference with minor presentation issues.



**Partial:** Partly inappropriate or misinterpreted inference.



**Insufficient:** Incorrect standard errors or inference.



## Interpretation — 10 points



**Excellent:** Reports units and scale: dollars, percentages, or percentage points; distinguishes geometric and arithmetic quantities.



**Adequate:** Correct interpretations with minor imprecision.



**Partial:** Vague interpretations or missing units.



**Insufficient:** Treats index coefficients as units or probabilities.



## Diagnostics — 10 points



**Excellent:** Connects residuals, dispersion, and influence evidence to analytical decisions.



**Adequate:** Adequate, interpreted diagnostics.



**Partial:** Mechanical diagnostics disconnected from decisions.



**Insufficient:** No diagnostics.



## Robustness — 10 points



**Excellent:** Interprets comparative alternatives for form, sample, missingness, extremes, and controls.



**Adequate:** Relevant alternatives interpreted well.



**Partial:** Weak or uninterpreted alternatives.



**Insufficient:** No robustness or search for a preferred result.



## Economic relevance and decision — 8 points



**Excellent:** Separates significance from relevance; compares net contribution and cost with uncertainty and states future-value assumptions.



**Adequate:** Correct calculation with partly developed uncertainty.



**Partial:** Incomplete calculation or disproportionate recommendation.



**Insufficient:** Decision based only on p-value or without costs.



## Causal limits and design — 7 points



**Excellent:** Distinguishes assignment effects under design assumptions from conditional app association and explains unobserved confounding.



**Adequate:** Correct distinction with brief explanation.



**Partial:** Incomplete or inconsistent distinction.



**Insufficient:** Claims causal app effect or ignores design.



## Reproducibility and communication — 5 points



**Excellent:** Restart and Run All works; relative paths and hashes; conclusions and recommendations are clearly linked to evidence.



**Adequate:** Reproducible notebook and adequate communication.



**Partial:** Reproduction needs repairs or communication is unclear.



**Insufficient:** Notebook is not reproducible.



## Optional extension



Omission does not lower the grade. A correct extension with graph, justified deterministic terms, cautious unit-root interpretation, annual growth, and business interpretation may earn up to five bonus points at instructor discretion; the final grade is capped at 100.



## Serious conceptual errors



Taking logs of zeros without an explicit decision; treating app association as causal; controlling visits without recognizing a changed question; reading nonlinear coefficients as probabilities or units; inappropriate usual standard errors with relevant heteroskedasticity or overdispersion; deleting extremes without investigation; p-value-driven selection; recommending coupons without cost comparison; or submitting a notebook that does not run can substantially reduce the relevant dimension score.



## Alternative solutions



A different solution can earn full credit when substantively justified, documented, consistently applied, and accompanied by its consequences. Agreement with a reference recommendation is not required.
