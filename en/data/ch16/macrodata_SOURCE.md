# Historical US macroeconomic data

203 quarters, 1959Q1–2009Q3. Local snapshot from statsmodels 0.14.5; source note records a December 2009 vintage. Real GDP is billions of chained 2005 dollars, seasonally adjusted at an annual rate. Inflation is annualized; unemployment is a percentage. Initial inflation is a missing-value placeholder and is excluded; initial GDP and unemployment are retained. Compiled by Skipper Seabold from FRED and BLS. Official documentation: https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/datasets/generated/macrodata.html . The project describes the dataset as public domain; this private package is not publication authorization. Quarterly unemployment aggregation is not specified in the note. No updated series is substituted.
